- Identified and eliminated race condition in [`backend/src/codestruct/jobs/executor.py`](file:///d:/REP/Codestruct/Codestruct/backend/src/codestruct/jobs/executor.py) where the worker thread previously transitioned the job state to `COMPLETED` in SQLite *before* writing the graph to `cache_results`.
- Moved `store_cache()` execution before `registry.transition()` to guarantee cache availability immediately upon any consumer observing a `COMPLETED` state.
